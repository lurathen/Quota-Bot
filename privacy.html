Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Staff Quota Bot processes information when it is added to and used in a Discord server.

Information the bot processes

To provide quota tracking, reminders, breaks, configuration, and welcome features, the bot may process and store Discord identifiers and configuration data, including:

  • Discord server (guild) IDs;
  • Discord user IDs;
  • Discord message IDs used to prevent duplicate counting;
  • weekly message counts;
  • role IDs, channel IDs, quota targets, timezone settings, and reminder settings;
  • break status and reminder timestamps; and
  • welcome-message configuration selected by authorized server staff.

Message content

The bot does not store the text or content of Discord messages. It observes eligible messages only as necessary to increment quota counts and prevent the same message from being counted twice.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the bot's features, including calculating staff activity totals, displaying quota progress, sending reminders, managing breaks, enforcing configuration access, and sending configured welcome messages.

Data retention

Weekly count and message-tracking records are periodically pruned, with old tracking records generally retained for roughly two weeks. Some configuration information, break status, and reminder timing data may remain until it is changed, removed, or the bot's stored server data is deleted.

Sharing and sale of data

The bot does not sell personal information. Data is not intentionally shared with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be processed by the hosting provider and Discord as necessary to operate the service.

Server administrators

Server owners and roles specifically authorized by the server owner may be able to view or change quota-related information through the bot's commands. Discord's own permissions and policies also apply.

Data removal

A server owner may stop further processing by removing the bot from the server. Requests concerning deletion of stored bot data should be directed to the bot operator through the Discord community in which the bot is provided.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored bot data, but no online service or hosting platform can guarantee absolute security.

Children

The bot is designed for use within Discord communities and is not intended to independently collect information from children outside the normal operation of a Discord server. Server operators are responsible for using the bot in accordance with Discord's rules and applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-removal requests, contact the operator of Staff Quota Bot through the Discord server where the bot is being used.
